# Project Proposal: Peer Feedback

Peer feedback on the SwoopSwap project proposal, copied verbatim from Canvas. The number is the reviewer's row number and the score is what they gave (blank where none was given).

## What came up most

Reviewers agreed the problem is real: the May dumpster waste followed by fall rebuying was the part of the pitch that landed, and the Emory-email verification plus tag/follow notifications read as a clear improvement over the GroupMe and Facebook buy/sell groups. Several people also called out the decision to leave payments off the platform as a good one.

The suggestions cluster around three gaps we should address before building:

1. **Trust and safety.** This was the most repeated concern (#78, #86, #89, #93, #96, #98). Reviewers want an actual verification and dispute flow, a reporting feature beyond seller ratings, and more required detail on listings (condition, time used, close-up photos of defects) since a small community means few ratings to rely on.
2. **The move-out / move-in timing gap.** #80, #81, #83, #85, #89, and #94 all point out that sellers list in May and buyers show up in August. Concrete ideas: a pickup window and latest pickup date on each listing, a filter by collection time, nudging sellers to list well before move-out, and stating explicitly that summer storage is out of scope.
3. **Meetup logistics and the agent's role.** #78 and #93 want to know how meetup spots are chosen (fixed, verified campus locations like bluelights, or user-picked) and how users signal their preferences. #78 also asks where the ReAct agent fits in the full flow and suggests evaluating it against plain browsing on search time and steps to complete a transaction.

Smaller one-off ideas: profile badges with major and grad year, a rent option, a 'first come first serve' tag (#87); shipping and expansion to other campuses (#82); tracking completed vs. failed exchanges during a pilot (#94).
